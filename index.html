Game mechanics

How it works

Three steps. The moment you start running, the map becomes the board.

01

Encircle on foot

Open the app and run the streets. Let your GPS trail form a closed line — a polygon.

02

Paint the map

When you return to your start point, the enclosed area is claimed in your color. The area is added to your score.

03

Hold and expand

Other runners can cut your territory by running along its edge. Defend it, or grow into the next neighborhood.
